## Artemide Duo v0.26

**Controller always in the stream**
- Controller, hardware keyboard and mouse input always go to the stream, also after tapping the keyboard panel on the other screen (the panel hands everything over).
- LB + RB shortcuts and G / D / Off work right from the start of the stream.

**Smoother motion**
- Screen changes are a soft cross dissolve (Premiere / PlayStation style) with just a breath of zoom; stream open and close are slower and softer.
- App list: covers cross-dissolve when you move through the list, and the selected cover fills the background large, blurred and faint (PS5 style).
- Lifted buttons and the stream-start glow are no longer cut off; full-width rows keep a focus ring instead of growing.

**Other**
- App icon: the original Artemide logo in Ion cyan on a dark background.
